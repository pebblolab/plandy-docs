iPhone, iPad, Mac, 그리고 iPhone Duo 같은 접는 화면까지. 화면 크기에 맞춰 배치가 바뀌어요.

Plandy — Calendar & to-dos you can just type

Plandy brings your iPhone Calendar and Reminders together in one place. Type something like “내일 오후 3시 회의” (tomorrow 3 PM meeting) and the date, time, and repeat are filled in for you (Korean natural language). Widgets, Apple Watch, Siri & Shortcuts, and Korean public holidays included. No account, no ads, no tracking — your data stays on your device and in your iCloud.
